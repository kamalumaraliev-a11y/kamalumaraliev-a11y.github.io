StudioMD on the map: the scan evidence

Every number in the growth snapshot about Google Maps comes from these scans, run on the night of 20 October 2026. Each map is one search term checked from 49 points on a grid around a studio, organic results only (no ads). The number in each circle is where StudioMD ranked at that spot: green is the map pack (top 3), yellow 4th to 10th, red 11th to 20th, and 20+ means not in the first twenty.

Three measures appear under each map. Top 3 at N of 49 is how many spots put StudioMD in the pack, the organic version of impression share. Seen in the top 20 is how many spots show it at all. Average rank where seen ignores the spots where it did not appear, so it flatters; a studio seen at 1 spot with an average rank of 19 is invisible.

Does "near me" change the picture?

A fair question about the method: people type "botox near me", not "botox". Two extra scans at SoHo the next morning say the bare term and the "near me" term land in the same place.

For comparison, SoHo on the bare terms: botox top 3 at 0 of 49, seen at 10, average rank 12.6; med spa top 3 at 0 of 49, seen at 13, average rank 11.4.

Method

Tool: Local Falcon geo-grid scans, 7 by 7 grid, 49 points. Radius from the studio: 0.75 mi at SoHo and Midtown, 1 mi at Brooklyn and Jackson Heights, 2 mi at Garden City, White Plains and Larchmont, 2.5 mi at Darien, 3 mi at Mendham and Glen Mills, so the grid covers the area a patient would actually drive from. Terms: the category word (med spa), the three biggest lines on the menu (laser hair removal, botox, coolsculpting) and the GLP-1 line (weight loss clinic). 50 Google Maps scans, 3 Apple Maps scans and 9 AI scans in all; the closed Jackson Heights listing was scanned to confirm it no longer appears anywhere.
